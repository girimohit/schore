
# SCHORE

**Schore is a modern school management platform designed to bring everyday academic workflows into one simple, connected experience.**

It helps schools, teachers, and students manage essential academic activities while allowing each school to configure its own features and visual identity.

## What Schore Offers

- Student and faculty management
- Classes, sections, subjects, and academic years
- Attendance
- Homework & assignments
- Timetable
- Notices & announcements
- Exams & results
- Student remarks
- School-specific branding and feature configuration

## School-Specific Experience

Schore is built around the idea that schools don't all operate in exactly the same way.

Each school can have its own:

- Logo and visual identity
- Primary and secondary colors
- Enabled modules
- Academic configuration
- School-specific settings

The underlying platform remains consistent while the experience adapts to each school.

## One Platform, Multiple Schools

Schore is designed to support multiple schools while keeping their data, users, configuration, and access properly isolated.

Schools are provisioned through controlled administration rather than unrestricted public registration.

## Mobile First

The primary experience is designed for mobile users, particularly students and faculty who interact with the platform throughout their school day.

The application focuses on keeping common tasks quick and straightforward rather than turning everyday school activities into complicated workflows.

## Project Status

Schore is currently under active development, with the initial focus on building a reliable core experience around the most frequently used school workflows.
